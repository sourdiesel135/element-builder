## Risk Analysis & Mitigation

### Technical Risks
❌ **AI API costs scaling** → Mitigation: Hybrid on-device + cloud approach
❌ **Battery drain from AI processing** → Mitigation: Smart caching, background limits
❌ **Complex consciousness algorithms on mobile** → Mitigation: Start simple, iterate

### Market Risks
❌ **Niche audience** → Mitigation: Start with proven mental wellness market
❌ **"Woo-woo" perception** → Mitigation: Lead with psychology/science, add mystical layer
❌ **Retention challenges** → Mitigation: Daily engagement hooks, habit formation

### Business Risks
❌ **Development costs** → Mitigation: MVP approach, phased rollout
❌ **Competitive response** → Mitigation: Strong unique positioning (consciousness angle)
❌ **User acquisition costs** → Mitigation: Organic growth through unique value prop
